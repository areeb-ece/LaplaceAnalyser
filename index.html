Syntax and keys

What you can type

TypeMeaning

y', y'', y^(4), dy/dt, d^2y/dt^2Derivatives of , in an equation: .
1/2, pi, e, sqrt(2)Numbers, anywhere a number is wanted: .

What each mode takes

EquationA linear equation in with number coefficients, up to order 8, such as y'' + 2y' + 5y = e^(-t) cos(2t), with its starting values.
Function Sums and products of numbers, powers of , exponentials, sines and cosines.
Transform A ratio of polynomials in whose denominator has the higher degree, such as (s + 3)/((s + 1)(s^2 + 4)).

In a maths box

/Starts a fraction. What stands just before it goes on top; select more first to put all of it there.
^Starts a power. Everything typed next is raised, until you step out.
Space →Steps out of a power or a fraction. ↑ and ↓ go between the top and the bottom of a fraction.
(Brings its closing bracket with it, in grey until you type it.
⌫At the start of a fraction or a power, takes it apart again.
Ctrl + ZUndo. With Shift, redo. Copy and paste work on plain text, written the way the first table shows.

Keys

SpacePlay or pause time.
← → ↑ ↓On the surface or the pole-zero map: move the probe (hold Shift for bigger steps). On the time plot: move the time cursor. On a gap between panels: resize them.
Alt + arrowsOn the surface: turn the camera.
+ −On the surface or the pole-zero map: zoom.
EscRestore a maximized panel.
